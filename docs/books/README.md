## Scientific foundations

The Scientific Inquiry sourcebook is the methodological and historical spine of this domain. It links the [scientific method](../scientific-method.md), [global history of science](../history-of-science.md), [research methods atlas](../research-methods-atlas.md), [reproducibility and open science](../reproducibility-and-open-science.md), and existing [scientific integrity and validation](../scientific-integrity.md) guide.

## First facility specimen

[Teaching and Research Campus — implementation specification](../specimens/teaching-research-campus.md) defines the first original, fictional campus to implement. It exercises visitor navigation, supervised learning, research review, instrument service, resource decisions, safety governance, and trusted-host boundaries without pretending those runtime capabilities already exist.

## LOD 3 facility gallery

- **Frankenstein's laboratory** — fictional source adaptation; scientific ambition, personhood, consent, and responsibility.
- **Groom Lake / Area 51** — documented public history; aerospace testing, secrecy, declassification, and evidence.
- **CERN / Large Hadron Collider** — documented science campus; accelerator chain, detectors, controls, and international collaboration.
- **NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory** — documented institution; robotic exploration, spacecraft integration, mission operations, and deep-space communications.
- **European Molecular Biology Laboratory** — documented distributed institution; six sites, research services, training, and shared governance.
- **Los Alamos National Laboratory** — documented multidisciplinary national laboratory; public mission, research portfolio, and institutional accountability.
- **Original speculative facilities** — solar-system observatories, deep-time Earth science, living systems, materials research, interstellar stations, and a civilization-scale knowledge archive.

The facility profiles distinguish documented fact, reconstruction, literary fiction, and original speculation. They do not imply access to restricted sites or claim unimplemented runtime capabilities.
